A few questions

What does the 25% cover?

Listing, pricing, guest care, and management. Cleaning and maintenance are billed at cost, with no markup.

No lock-in. We discuss notice, existing reservations and account access in the agreement. Listing and review arrangements depend on the platform and current setup.

Can I still use my home?

Of course. Block the dates you want; we work around your stays.
