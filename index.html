Can I still use my home?+

Yes. Block the dates you want for yourself, family, or friends. We manage guest bookings around those stays.

What does your management fee cover?+

Our fee is 25% of gross booking value for listing, pricing, guest care, and management. Cleaning and maintenance are billed separately at cost, without a markup. We can walk through the details for your home before you decide.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?+

There is no lock-in. You keep your listing and reviews. We will walk you through the management agreement and transition arrangements before you get started.

Where do you manage homes?+

We manage vacation rentals across North Carolina, South Carolina, and Georgia. Share your town or property address and we will confirm whether we can support your home. If you own elsewhere, you are welcome to enquire too.

Is the rental estimate a guarantee?+

No. An estimate is a starting point for a conversation. Actual earnings depend on your property, available dates, seasonality, demand, and operating costs. We will discuss what those factors mean for your home.

I already have a manager. Can we talk?+

Of course. Tell us what is working and what you would like to change. We can discuss your home and what a transition could look like before you make a decision.
