Your privacy.

Your enquiry

When you request a rental estimate, we send the property and contact details you provide to Blue Sky Village through its existing Proptonomy lead service. The team uses those details to respond to your enquiry. The service also sends an email notification to the team.

Submitting this form does not add you to a marketing list.

Comparing our website versions

We are comparing two versions of this website. Your browser stores a random identifier and your assigned version for up to 90 days, so repeat visits show the same version.

We record the version, browser identifier, and the time of visits, enquiry starts, and completed enquiries. These measurement records do not include your property address, name, email, or phone number. The version is also attached to a submitted enquiry so we can compare the enquiries each version produces.

Clearing this site's browser storage removes your saved assignment. If browser storage is unavailable, the original site remains available without participating in the test.
