The details between beach days matter.

A beach home brings a different set of hosting questions. Where do guests leave sandy gear? How is outdoor furniture checked? What are the parking instructions, and who can reach the property if something needs attention?

Blue Sky Village’s portfolio includes a Folly Beach property. For a new owner, we begin by learning how the home is used today and which parts of hosting you want to hand over. Your own beach weekends should be part of that conversation.

Bring these details about your home

  • Your address, rental business license and registration status.
  • Current listing, booking calendar and any upcoming owner stays.
  • Parking spaces, outdoor areas and turnover arrangements.
  • Your expectations for maintenance approval and local response.

The license and registration are separate checks.

Folly Beach’s official guidance distinguishes rental business licensing from rental registration. It also explains different license categories and restrictions on investor licenses. A listing going live, a home purchase or a change of manager should not be treated as automatic permission to rent.

Confirm your property’s category and status directly with the city. Keep those records with your operating information so an agreed hosting plan starts from the right permissions.

Local reference, checked September 13, 2026: City of Folly Beach short-term rental guidance. Use the city’s current requirements for your address.

Build the plan around the whole home.

For a Folly Beach property, the management conversation should cover interior and exterior turnovers, salt and sand exposure, weather preparation, guest instructions and an agreed route for maintenance decisions.

Blue Sky Village’s advertised fee is 25% of gross booking value for listing, pricing, guest care and management. Cleaning and maintenance are billed at cost without markup. We discuss the responsibilities and costs for your home before you commit.

A useful rental estimate considers owner dates and seasonal changes. One strong summer month should not be multiplied by twelve and presented as an annual forecast.

A few things owners ask

Can I keep dates for my own beach trips?

Yes. Share the dates you want to reserve so they can be included in the availability plan and rental estimate.

I am buying a Folly Beach rental. Is the existing license enough?

Do not assume the seller’s license gives you ongoing permission. Ask the city about the rules for the purchase and your intended use before relying on a rental forecast.

Can I enquire if another manager runs my home?

Yes. Bring the current agreement, reservation schedule and license details. We can discuss what a careful transition would involve.
