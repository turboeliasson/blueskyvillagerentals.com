# Blue Sky Village: the Southern lakeside guestbook

The brand should feel like a place people belong: sunny, personal, a little nostalgic, and confident in its hospitality. The expressive serif wordmark, hand-painted lake, and postcards carry the character; the enquiry flow remains simple and direct.

## Palette and type

- Butter paper: #fcf5e6
- Deep blue ink: #243f47
- Weathered lake blue: #345e73
- Sun yellow: #efc95f
- Pale yellow: #f4dc94
- Display and wordmark: Young Serif
- Personal notes: Caveat, used sparingly
- Supporting copy and controls: DM Sans

The original heron badge stays. Property photos remain unaltered and are presented as postcards. Generated scenery is clearly painterly and is decorative brand art, never property evidence. Avoid unverified testimonials, ratings, revenue claims, or invented team members.

## Generated assets

Generated with the subscription-backed built-in imagegen tool, one request per asset. No API key or separately billed model API was used.
